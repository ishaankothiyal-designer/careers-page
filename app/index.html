needs strong
support.

Mission Elevate +

Internal opportunities help frontline builders take their experience into new problems and new parts of the business.

Founders’ Awards +

08 / An honest exchange

If you want a ladder,
this is the wrong company.

Some people want a clear ladder and a conventional title. Others want harder problems and more ambiguity. Both are fair choices.

01

Agency comes
with consequence.

Owning the decision means staying with its consequences. Bring your judgement, explain your reasoning, and learn openly when a sincere call fails.

02

Flat can fail.
Call it out.

Aspiration and reality can differ. When informal hierarchy gets in the way, bring the gap into the conversation and help change it.

03

Some work keeps
its hierarchy.

Some operations need clear decision rights and accountability. The way the work runs will vary by role.
